Forest Shuffle: Dartmoor

Posted on Jan 20, 2024

Fun game! I bought the Dartmoor version because my colleague Jurgen already has the base game, and because they only had this version in Dutch at my local game store. Playing this game in Dutch is a must in my opinion. Some card rules are just complex enough to feel more comfortable in your native toungue. And more importantly, Dutch animal names are usually very silly and I wouldn't want to miss that.

What I like

  • Variable ending, you can never know exactly on which turn the game will end.
  • Scores are hidden until the end. Knowing early on that you're losing badly isn't fun.
  • Quite forgiving. You have enough room to go for multiple separate builds. In contrast, in Everdell you need to be much more selective with what you invest into.
  • Animals! And how their mechanics mimic their real-world counterparts. Like how a cuckoo must be in a tree with another bird, and how you can play multiple rabbits at once.
  • Cards as currency. It simplifies the game quite a lot, in Everdell you need to juggle cards AND four resource types.

What I dislike

  • Two player game tends to drag on a bit. I went for one big play. Once I completed it, I had like 10 more turns left. I might have liked a little more sense of urgency. A possible reason could be that this game has moors, which partly replace trees. And trees speed up the game, since for every played tree a card from the pile gets drawn. I bought this game because I wanted a quicker Everdell. While it IS simpler, it's not quicker.
  • In addition to trees, you can now also play moors. They're placed horizontally instead of vertically, which means that your forest will look quite messy. Not a real issue though.
  • The box could be 50% smaller. The game feels big enough to warrant a big box, but I always appreciate games that are as compact as they could be.
  • Counting points takes longer than any other game I've ever played. Not a big problem but still remarkable.
